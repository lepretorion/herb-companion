Understanding Herb Lifespans

Annuals Cilantro, Basil

Live for one season. Sowing new seeds every 3-4 weeks guarantees non-stop fresh harvests.

Biennials Parsley

Lives for 2 years. Leaves are best in year 1; flowers in year 2 make leaves bitter.

Perennials Rosemary, Mint, Thyme, Sage, Oregano

Returns year after year. Prune regularly to prevent stems from turning overly woody.
